How We Support Each Other

Beyond just networking opportunities, our brotherhood provides concrete support for professional development. From resume reviews and interview preparation to mentorship and skill-building workshops, we help each other succeed in our respective fields.

Resume Workshops

Brothers with experience in recruiting regularly host resume workshops to help members craft professional resumes that stand out to employers.

Exclusive Networking Events

We host exclusive networking events with alumni and industry professionals to provide members with opportunities to connect and learn from experienced leaders.
