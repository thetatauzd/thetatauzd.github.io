# Brother Portal: officer guide

Everything runs at **thetatauzd.org/portal**. Sign in with your Google account. Your position decides which pages
appear under **Chapter Ops** in the header. If a page you need is missing, ask the Technology Chair to check your
position on User Management.

Every number the site uses (demerits per event, thresholds, dues, late fees, service requirement) comes from
**Chapter Settings**, so the site always follows the bylaws as they are written there.

This guide is public on GitHub. Never write a brother's name next to demerits, standing, money or notes here.

---

## This semester (Fall 2026): the Tracker sheet is the record

The chapter decided to keep the Google **Tracker** sheet as the record for Fall 2026. Nothing changes in how you do
your job this semester:

- **Officers** keep entering attendance, excuse rulings, adjustments, payments and service hours **on the Tracker
  sheet**, as before.
- **Brothers** keep using the **Google Forms** for excuses and service hours (Menu → *Request an excuse* and *Log
  service hours*, or the Forms card on the portal home). Their **Theta Tau Tracker** page reads the sheet directly, so
  it is always up to date.
- **The Chapter Ops pages are a preview.** They show a read-only copy of the sheet, so you can get used to them before
  the switch. Every page opens with a yellow note saying when the copy was made and by whom. There are no buttons
  that save anything: make every change on the sheet, and it shows on the site after the next refresh.
- **Refresh**: an admin presses *Refresh from sheet*. The copy also refreshes itself when an admin opens a preview
  page and it is more than a day old. A refresh takes under a minute: stay on the page until it says it is done
  (the browser asks before you leave). If the copy looks out of date, ask an admin.
- **My Tracker (preview)** (Chapter Ops menu) shows your own record as the site computes it from the copy. Brothers
  do not see it.
- Only officers see the **Officer preview** card on the portal home and the Chapter Ops menu.

What each page shows this semester:

| Page | You can see | Not this semester |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | each event's marks from the sheet as Present/Absent, with excuse letters and a count | adding events, roll call, saving |
| Standards Board | the dashboard, excuse requests with their rulings (reasons: Standards Chair and admins only), adjustments, roll-over | approving, denying, adjustments, standing overrides |
| Treasurer | dues, balances with history, late fees, **Export to Excel** | charges, payments, plans, waivers, reversals, buy-outs |
| Service Hours | entries waiting for approval, progress, the week board | approving, bulk credit |
| Member record | one person's record, the parts your position may read | editing service entries |
| Marshal | the class, readiness and meeting marks | creating a class, exams, initiated, de-pledge |
| Semester Setup | a closed notice | everything (it opens after the switch) |
| Chapter Settings | every number, read-only, plus the sheet's own numbers for this term | editing (admins too) |

The totals on the preview pages use the sheet's own numbers for this term (for example 8 service hours), so they match
the sheet's dashboard. If a total on the site differs from the sheet, tell the Technology Chair: it usually means a
name is spelled two ways on the sheet.

The sections below describe how each page works **after the switch to This site** (planned for Spring 2027).

## Scribe: Attendance

This semester: take attendance on the Tracker sheet. The Attendance page shows the copied marks only.

**Every chapter or event** (after the switch)
1. Open **Attendance** and tap tonight's event. (Events are scheduled at the start of the semester on Semester Setup.
   If it's missing, add it under "Add an event".)
2. Tap **Everyone present**, then tap **Absent** next to each empty seat.
3. Press **Save attendance**. The confirm box lists everyone marked absent: read it out loud before you press OK.

**The letters next to names**
- **E**: Standards approved an excuse. If they aren't there, still mark them **Absent**. They are charged the
  excused rate, not the unexcused one.
- **L**: approved to arrive late or leave early. Mark them **Present**.
- **?**: Standards hasn't ruled yet. Mark what you see.

**If something goes wrong**
- Your phone reloaded or locked: open the event again and say **OK** to "Restore it?". Your taps are kept on the phone
  until you save.
- Wrong mark after saving: open the event, fix it, save again. The latest save wins.
- Never take roll for the same event on two phones at once.

## Standards Chair: Standards Board

This semester: rule on excuses and enter adjustments on the Tracker sheet. The Standards Board shows the copy, with
reasons for you and admins only.

**Every week** (after the switch)
1. **Excuse requests** → *To decide* → *By event*.
2. Each card shows what is at stake, whether it came in on time (24 h), how many free absences they have left and
   how many requests they've sent. Open the photos if any.
3. Tick routine requests and press **Approve selected**, or approve and deny one at a time. Add a note when you deny.
4. Sickness: the "no doctor's note" pill means the bylaws want a note.
5. "Unlisted event": pick the real event in **Link to an event…** before approving, or the approval can't count.

**Adjustments**: always give a reason. Credits are negative (Lock In −2), hearing demerits positive.

**Undo**
- A ruling: *Decided* tab → **Change to approved/denied**.
- An adjustment: add the opposite adjustment, reason "correction of <date> entry". Entries are never deleted.

Only the Standards Chair (and admins) can read excuse reasons. Other officers who open this page see the dashboard
read-only.

## Treasurer: Treasurer

This semester: record payments and fines on the Tracker sheet, as before. The Treasurer page shows balances and
**Export to Excel** still works.

**When the national invoice comes (Oct 15 / Mar 1)** (after the switch)
1. Set the due date (the bylaws make dues late one day after).
2. Press **Charge dues to all active** once. Anyone who already has a dues charge is skipped automatically. Press
   **Charge PNM dues** separately.

**Fees** (BEWE $135, pinning $30, initiation $90…): *Charge a fee or event*, name it, amount, due date, tick people,
**Charge selected**.

**Payments**: open the person, type the amount, pick the method, **Record payment**.

**Late fees** are added automatically by the bylaws ladder ($5, $5, then $10 a week). Never add one by hand.
A charge marked **Late fee owed** means the dues are paid and only the fee is left. It costs no demerits.
**Waive** waives everything still owed on that charge, fee included.

**Mistakes**: press **reverse** on the wrong entry and enter it again. The ledger is never edited.

**Buy-outs**: only after Standards confirms bad standing. **Record buy-out paid** charges and pays the fine and resets
the demerits.

**Budget**: still the Budget Google Sheet. **Export to Excel** gives you balances to paste in.

## Community Service Chair: Service Hours

This semester: confirm hours on the Tracker sheet. Brothers keep sending hours through the Google Form.

  that aren't one of the required events.

  **Credit selected**.

## Marshal: Marshal

This semester: the Marshal page is read-only, and pledge records are not copied from the sheet.

After the switch:
1. After bid day, make sure every PNM is approved as **PNM** on User Management (ask the Technology Chair).
2. **Create class**: label, start date, target initiation, one line per meeting (`Meeting 1, 2026-09-08`).

5. At initiation press **initiated**, then tell the Technology Chair to set them **Active** the same night. Until
   then they aren't on roll call, don't pay brother dues and can't vote.

## Every exec officer: Family Tree

- Menu → **Family Tree**. Every brother can look; exec (Regent, Vice-Regent, Scribe, Treasurer, Corresponding
  Secretary, Marshal, Standards Chair) can edit. Chairs can be given the `tree` capability on Chapter Settings once it
  opens again after the switch.
- **After initiation**: open the big, press **+ Add a little**, type the name, roll number, major, choose the new
  pledge class (**+ New class** adds it once for everyone) and a photo (any phone picture; it is cropped square and
  shrunk). A brother with two bigs gets a **Co-big** (dashed line).
- **Fix a line**: open the person → **Edit** → change *Big*. A loop (someone their own grand-big) is refused.
- **Family names**: open a family's root (the top person) → Edit → *Family name* and *Family colour*.
- **Export picture**: whole chapter or one family, with a title and background, as a PNG for Instagram, the
  rush slideshow or a poster. On a phone, export one family at a time (iPhones cannot draw the whole chapter in one
  picture); use a laptop for the full poster. Officers can choose black pills for resigned and removed brothers. The
  default draws everyone alike, which is the safe choice for anything you post.
- **Backup**: Export picture → *Download a backup file*, once a semester. When an officer downloads it, the file has
  standing and private notes, so keep it in an officers-only Drive folder, never in a shared or public one. The same
  file restores the tree on the page if it is ever emptied.
- **Offices held** show as the official signs (XX! past Regent, W! current Marshal …) on the pill and the card; set
  them in Edit → *Offices held*. The Brother List details (full name, minor, graduation, exec roles) are under a
  fold in the editor.
- **Someone resigns or is removed**: Edit → *Standing (officers only)*. They stay in the tree so their littles keep
  their big. Never delete them. Only officers see the black pill, the standing and private notes. Other brothers see
  an ordinary pill.
- **Still check by eye** after the first fill: the Drive **Brother List** was the source of truth, so roll numbers and
  classes that were wrong on the poster are fixed, and the Drive big/little match sheets (Spring 2022 to Fall 2025)
  confirmed the lines. A few brothers on the Brother List but not on the poster have no big yet. A brother who
  transferred in has no Zeta Delta roll number. Two removed brothers whose names are withheld are kept as numbered
  placeholder pills so their littles' lines stay whole. Some Alpha founders drawn one above the other on the poster
  were kept as big and little. Ask the Technology Chair if you need to know who is who.

## Technology Chair (admin)

- **User Management**: approve sign-ups, set statuses (the confirm box explains what each one means), edit positions
  after elections, **rename** people. It works as usual this semester.
- **Portal Links**: change the home-page links (new Google Form each semester, etc.). While the sheet is the record,
  the excuse and service tiles open the Google Forms by themselves.
- After changing what a position can do on Chapter Settings (after the switch), press **Refresh everyone's
  permissions** on User Management (or re-save each holder). Until then they keep their old permissions.
- **Refresh from sheet** (Admin menu): press **Check without saving** first, then **Refresh from sheet**.
  - The report says how many demerit totals on the site match the sheet's DemeritDashboard. A total that differs
    usually means the sheet itself has a typo, most often a name spelled two ways.
  - **Names the site could not match**: pick the right account, or "No portal account, stop asking". The choice is
    saved for every later refresh.
  - **Roster status and User Management disagree**: if the Roster is right, fix the status on User Management. The
    refresh never changes accounts.
  - It also runs on its own when an admin opens a preview page and the copy is over a day old.
  - Leaving a page while a refresh runs stops it partway (the browser asks first). The page lets go of it as it
    closes, the yellow note says "The last refresh stopped partway", and the next visit to a preview page makes
    the copy again. If the browser was killed or the connection was lost, the refresh keeps its hold for a while:
    you can take it over after 2 minutes (below), anyone else after 10 minutes.
  - "A refresh you started on another page or tab is running": if that page is closed, press the button again
    once the refresh is 2 minutes old and this page takes it over. Another admin's refresh: wait for it to finish
    (at most 10 minutes).
  - If the top card says "Old version. Redeploy Gateway.gs", follow `portal/apps-script/README.md` → Redeploy (a new
    version of the existing deployment, never a new deployment).
- **Family Tree, first time**: the tree starts empty. The database rules for the family tree must be published first.
  Then open Family Tree and press **Restore from a backup file**, and pick the private copy of the poster file from
  the officers-only Drive folder. Standing and notes go to the officers-only part. Never put that file, or any file
  with brothers' personal data, in the repository: it is public.
- **Admins can read every excuse reason and photo** (deaths, family emergencies). Keep exactly two admins: the
  Technology Chair and the Standards Chair.
- **The switch to This site** (planned for Spring 2027): follow "Switching the record to the site" in
  `semester-checklist.md`.
- Follow `semester-checklist.md` each semester, and its admin-succession section before you graduate.
