1 Start a new semester

Attendance, excuses, demerits, fees and service hours are all filed under the semester, so starting a new one gives everyone a clean slate. The old semester stays on record. Only what the Standards procedures carry forward comes along: service shortfalls become roll-over demerits.

Before you start: check who is Active, Co-op, Abroad, Inactive or Alumni in User Management. Dues and roll call follow those statuses.

3 Old photos
