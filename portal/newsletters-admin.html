Publish a newsletter

1. Upload the newsletter PDF to Google Drive (chapter shared drive works well).
2. Right-click the file → Share → General access → "Anyone with the link" (Viewer).
3. Copy the link and paste it below. It goes live on the public Newsletters page immediately.
