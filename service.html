Most of our service is hands-on: showing local kids what engineers do, fixing up houses and volunteering with Columbia organizations that need the help.

STEM Outreach Events

Two events a year where local K-12 students build something, test it and take it home.

Theta Tau hosts two STEM outreach events a year for local K-12 students, where students are exposed to engineering and science in a fun and engaging way.

Brothers plan each event over a semester with local schools and community partners, then run the stations on the day.

Making a Difference in the Community

House renovations, shelters and partner organizations around Columbia.

Soda City Science

Find our table at Soda City Market, where we talk to parents and families about engineering and advertise our outreach events!
