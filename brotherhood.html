Rush and pledging

After rush you join a pledge class and spend the semester getting to know every brother. You get a big, a family and a group of people taking the same classes you are.

Meet Brothers Social Events Brotherhood Dinners

Away weekends

Beach weekend, mountain weekend, formal, semi-formal, tubing, Carowinds and the state fair happen every year. They are where most of the friendships in this chapter start.

Mountain Retreats Group Adventures Team Building Lifelong Memories
